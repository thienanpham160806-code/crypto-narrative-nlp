# X4 synthetic validation

stationarity.csv reports ADF (unit-root null) and KPSS (stationary-level null) on the longest contiguous development segment. Rejection is a diagnostic, not a claim of profitable predictability; KPSS p-values are bounded by its lookup table.

robustness.csv compares horizons 1/3/7, full/pre/post January 2025 samples, momentum, raw document count, and narrative shares. The level dummy controls the known January 2025 coverage break; it cannot repair missing news or prove stable measurement.

robustness_status.csv marks source-weighted and price-excluded aggregation pending. Supply separately rebuilt parquet via --source-weighted-text and --exclude-price-text; do not approximate document-weighted sentiment from daily averages.

Holm/BH corrections use each exported table as a family, excluding missing p-values. Corrections do not remove confounding or turn predictive associations into causal effects.

The original X1-X3 metadata describes the original run. X4 tables and p-value corrections were generated on 2026-10-16 with seed 42 and config/config.yaml. ML forecasts were moved unchanged; OOS DM corrections were added without retuning.
